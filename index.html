A salon appointment without leaving the house

Most people don't stop going to the hairdresser because they stop caring. They stop because getting there, parking, waiting and getting back takes half a day they haven't got.

I come to you with the chair, the colours, the tools and the towels. You get one hairdresser, one client, and a cup of tea in your own kitchen.

Cutting

Restyles, trims and everything in between, cut to suit how you actually wear your hair day to day.

Colour

Roots, all-over colour and highlights, done at your kitchen table with no salon waiting room.

Occasions

Weddings, parties and big days, with styling done at home so you can get ready without rushing.
